Brand Essence 02
01 · Brand Essence
What Visa Forte is.

The practice does not sell hope. It engineers passage. Every brand decision — mark, colour, typeface, tone — is derived from this single statement.

Brand Persona

The most knowledgeable colleague in the room — completely calm, completely authoritative, completely on the client's side.

Positioning

Premium Canadian immigration documentation consultancy based in Secunderabad, India. 20+ years of domain expertise. Output: McKinsey-quality guidance at human scale.

The Tagline
Engineered for Passage
.
Engineered

Not guided, not helped, not assisted. Engineered. The word carries precision, process, and mastery. It removes hope from the equation and replaces it with methodology.

Passage

Not arrival, not immigration, not relocation. Passage — the threshold moment, the crossing. It is architectural and timeless, free of bureaucratic language.

Brand Values
Authority

Earned through two decades of documentation expertise. Never asserted — demonstrated.

Precision

Every word, every document, every proportion is deliberate. The mark's ratios are not decorative — they are architectural constants.

Calm

Immigration is stressful. The brand is not. Prussian and Pearl are the colours of settled intelligence, not urgent action.

Colour usage hierarchy
Dominant (≥ 60%)

Forte Prussian is the authority surface. Primary backgrounds, the mark, wordmark, and body copy all default to Prussian.

Supporting (≥ 30%)

Forte Pearl is breathing room. It is the ground on which Prussian elements sit. Light backgrounds, paper, card stock.

Accent (≤ 10%)

Forte Saffron is used exactly three times: the keystone line in the mark, the separator rule, and the period. Never as a background fill.

Usage Rules 08
06 · Usage Rules
Clear space. Minimum size.
What not to do.
Clear space

Maintain a minimum clear space around all lockups equal to the height of the threshold mark's lintel. No other element — text, image, line, edge — may enter this zone.

Minimum sizes
Primary horizontal lockup min 160px / 42mm
Primary stacked lockup min 80px / 21mm
Secondary lockup min 120px / 32mm
Threshold mark alone min 16px / 4mm
What not to do
✕ Don't stretch

Never scale the mark non-uniformly. The 4:5 ratio is an architectural constant.

✕ Don't fill with saffron

Saffron is never a dominant fill. The mark is always Prussian or Pearl.

✕ Don't rotate

The mark must always sit vertically. It is a standing gateway, not a decorative element.

✕ No drop shadows

No shadows, glows, or embossing effects. The mark relies on form, not effects.

✕ No gradients

Never place the logo on gradient backgrounds. Use only Prussian, Pearl, White, or Black.

VISA FORTE
✕ Don't recreate

Always use the provided SVG files. Never retype or recreate the wordmark in any other application.
