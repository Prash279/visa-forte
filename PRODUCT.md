# Product

## Register

both — brand for public marketing surfaces (/, /about, /services, /contact, /refund-policy, /privacy-policy); product for authenticated surfaces (/portal, /admin, /booking, /intake, /activate, /login, /signup). Per task, the surface in focus determines which register applies.

## Users

**Public site users:** Indian professionals who arrive via referral or WhatsApp recommendation. They have a high trust baseline before they land — someone they know vouched for Visa Forte. They are not researching from scratch; they are looking to confirm the quality of what they've heard and move to the next step quickly. The job to be done: validate trust, understand the service fit, and book.

**Portal users (authenticated clients):** Clients actively in their Canada PR process. They are mid-journey, at various stages of document preparation, application filing, or waiting for IRCC decisions. They want to feel that a competent expert is walking alongside them — not that they are navigating a form portal alone. The job to be done: know where they stand, take the next required action, and feel held throughout.

**Admin user:** Prashant Thirthingoth, sole consultant. Manages the pipeline, approves documents, sends messages, configures availability, and monitors bookings. Needs density and clarity over decoration.

## Product Purpose

Visa Forte is a premium Canadian immigration documentation consulting practice run by a 20+ year expert. The service helps Indian professionals understand their Canada PR eligibility, prepare documentation packages, and navigate the Express Entry and PR application process with precision. All output is framed as documentation education and eligibility guidance, not regulated immigration advice. Success looks like: a referred prospect books a consultation within minutes of landing on the site; an active client completes their document checklist without confusion or unnecessary back-and-forth.

## Brand Personality

Precise · Credible · Premium

Voice: authoritative without arrogance. The tone of a seasoned expert who has seen every scenario and knows exactly what to do next. Warm but never casual. Structured but never cold. Every word earns its place. "Engineered for Passage" is the permanent tagline — never alter, never omit.

## Anti-references

Avoid all of the following — none of them belong here:

- **Generic SaaS / startup:** Gradient hero sections, hero-metric templates (big number + label + gradient accent), ghost buttons on dark backgrounds, sans-serif everything, the Intercom / Notion / Lemon Squeezy aesthetic.
- **Cheap immigration portals:** Cramped forms, stock flag photography, blue-and-white government-adjacent styling, poor contrast, low typographic care.
- **Over-designed agency:** Excessive motion, neon splashes, parallax effects, floating decorative blobs, cursor effects. These undermine the trust signal this domain requires.
- **Old-school legal firm:** Gold serif wordmarks on dark navy, tombstone-style layouts, cold formality, Latin pretension. Stiff and off-putting.
- **Anything hard on the eyes:** Visual strain is a failure mode. Comfortable reading is a feature. Adequate line lengths, generous whitespace, restrained contrast — never force the reader to squint or fight the layout.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust confirmed, not built.** Referred visitors already believe in Visa Forte before they arrive. The design's job is to confirm that belief in seconds — not convince from zero. Signal credibility instantly through precision and restraint. Friction is the only way to lose them.

2. **Precision is the expertise signal.** The way information is organized communicates competence as loudly as any headline. Tight letter-spacing, structured hierarchy, and clean alignment tell the visitor this consultant runs a tight operation. Disorder signals incompetence.

3. **The portal is an expert's hand on the shoulder.** Every screen in the client portal should make the user feel accompanied — like a dedicated consultant is present, aware of where they are, and ready to guide the next step. Not a form. Not a checklist. A practice.

4. **Restraint over drama.** A confident practice doesn't need decorative flourishes. Resist the impulse to add motion, color, or structural complexity unless it earns its place functionally. Premium is often what you leave out.

5. **Easy on the eyes, always.** Comfortable reading is non-negotiable. Pearl backgrounds, soft contrast, generous spacing, and capped line lengths. Nothing that causes visual fatigue after 30 seconds. This applies to every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Priorities: sufficient color contrast ratios (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text), full keyboard navigation, screen reader-compatible markup, reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. No hard AAA requirement, but err toward AA+ where contrast is concerned given the Pearl-on-Prussian and Saffron-on-white combinations in use.
